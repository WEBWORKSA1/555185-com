What does 0437 mean in Chinese?

What 0437 means

0 = 你, 4 = 是, 3 = 神 (shén), 7 = 经 (jīng, loosely). A teasing jab between friends.

FAQ

What does 0437 mean in Chinese?

0437 means “You’re crazy” — it is read as 你是神经 (nǐ shì shén jīng). 0 = 你, 4 = 是, 3 = 神 (shén), 7 = 经 (jīng, loosely).

Can I use 0437 as a red envelope amount?

Chat slang — fun, not for gifts. It’s internet shorthand. Fine in messages and usernames, but it carries no particular luck as an amount.

How do you pronounce 0437?

Digit by digit in Mandarin: líng sì sān qī. The phrase it imitates is 你是神经 (nǐ shì shén jīng).
