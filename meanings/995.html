FAQ

What does 995 mean in Chinese?

995 means “Help me / save me” — it is read as 救救我 (jiù jiù wǒ). 99 = 救救 (save), 5 = 我.

Can I use 995 as a red envelope amount?

Chat slang — fun, not for gifts. It’s internet shorthand. Fine in messages and usernames, but it carries no particular luck as an amount.

How do you pronounce 995?
