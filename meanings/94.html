What 94 means

9 = 就, 4 = 是 (shì, ‘is’). One of the few slang uses where 4 means ‘yes’, not ‘death’.

94！我也这么想。
Exactly! I think so too.

FAQ

What does 94 mean in Chinese?

94 means “Exactly / that’s right” — it is read as 就是 (jiù shì). 9 = 就, 4 = 是 (shì, ‘is’).

Can I use 94 as a red envelope amount?

Chat slang — fun, not for gifts. It’s internet shorthand. Fine in messages and usernames, but it carries no particular luck as an amount.

How do you pronounce 94?

Digit by digit in Mandarin: jiǔ sì. The phrase it imitates is 就是 (jiù shì).
