FAQ

What does 9494 mean in Chinese?

9494 means “Totally! Absolutely right” — it is read as 就是就是 (jiù shì jiù shì). A doubled 94 for enthusiastic agreement.

Can I use 9494 as a red envelope amount?

Chat slang — fun, not for gifts. It’s internet shorthand. Fine in messages and usernames, but it carries no particular luck as an amount.

How do you pronounce 9494?

Digit by digit in Mandarin: jiǔ sì jiǔ sì. The phrase it imitates is 就是就是 (jiù shì jiù shì).
