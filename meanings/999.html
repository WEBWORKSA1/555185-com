FAQ

What does 999 mean in Chinese?

999 means “Everlasting” — it is read as 久久久 (jiǔ jiǔ jiǔ). Three 9s = forever and ever.

Can I use 999 as a red envelope amount?

Great for gifts, prices & numbers. This is a positive number in Chinese culture — safe and welcome as a red-envelope amount, price ending, phone number or plate.

How do you pronounce 999?

Digit by digit in Mandarin: jiǔ jiǔ jiǔ. The phrase it imitates is 久久久 (jiǔ jiǔ jiǔ).
