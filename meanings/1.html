Meaning & culture

In phone and plate numbers 1 is read ‘yāo’, which sounds like 要 (want / will). 1 also stands for 一 in 一生一世 (a lifetime), so it powers codes like 1314.

FAQ

What does the number 1 mean in Chinese?

1 is 一 / 幺 (yī / yāo). It sounds like 要 yào (want, will) · 一 yī (one, whole). In phone and plate numbers 1 is read ‘yāo’, which sounds like 要 (want / will). 1 also stands for 一 in 一生一世 (a lifetime), so it powers codes like 1314.

Is 1 a lucky number in China?

Neutral. No strong luck either way; meaning comes from the digits around it.
