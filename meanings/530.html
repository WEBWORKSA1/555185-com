What 530 means

5 = 我 (I), 3 = 想 (miss — sān/xiǎng), 0 = 你 (you). Common between long-distance couples.

FAQ

What does 530 mean in Chinese?

Can I use 530 as a red envelope amount?

Romantic — perfect between couples. Use it for anniversaries, 520 Day, proposals and wedding gifts. Less suited to business or elders.

How do you pronounce 530?

Digit by digit in Mandarin: wǔ sān líng. The phrase it imitates is 我想你 (wǒ xiǎng nǐ).
