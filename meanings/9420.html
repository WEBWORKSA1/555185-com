FAQ

What does 9420 mean in Chinese?

9420 means “It’s just that I love you” — it is read as 就是爱你 (jiù shì ài nǐ). 9 = 就 (just), 4 = 是 (is), 2 = 爱 (love), 0 = 你 (you).

Can I use 9420 as a red envelope amount?

Romantic — perfect between couples. Use it for anniversaries, 520 Day, proposals and wedding gifts. Less suited to business or elders.

How do you pronounce 9420?

Digit by digit in Mandarin: jiǔ sì èr líng. The phrase it imitates is 就是爱你 (jiù shì ài nǐ).
