What does 14 mean in Chinese?

14 = 要死 (yāo sǐ) — ‘Want to die’ — avoided.

yāo sǐ — ‘Want to die’ — avoided

What 14 means

Read phone-style as yāo-sì ≈ 要死 (‘going to die’). This is why many buildings skip the 14th floor and why 14 is avoided in plates, prices and red-envelope amounts.

电梯没有14楼。
The lift has no 14th floor.
⛔ Avoid for gifts and numbers
Its reading is negative. Don’t use it as a red-envelope amount, a price or a number you want to show off.
