What 886 means

88 + 6 (liù ≈ le). A casual sign-off in chats and forums.

明天见，886。
See you tomorrow, bye.
💬 Chat slang — fun, not for gifts
It’s internet shorthand. Fine in messages and usernames, but it carries no particular luck as an amount.

FAQ

What does 886 mean in Chinese?

886 means “Bye-bye (I’m off)” — it is read as 拜拜了 (bā bā le). 88 + 6 (liù ≈ le).

Can I use 886 as a red envelope amount?

Chat slang — fun, not for gifts. It’s internet shorthand. Fine in messages and usernames, but it carries no particular luck as an amount.

How do you pronounce 886?

Digit by digit in Mandarin: bā bā liù. The phrase it imitates is 拜拜了 (bā bā le).
