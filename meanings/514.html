What does 514 mean in Chinese?

What 514 means

5 = 我, 1 = 要, 4 = 死. A dramatic complaint; avoid in amounts and dates for gifts.

作业太多了，514。
So much homework — I’m dying.
⛔ Avoid for gifts and numbers
Its reading is negative. Don’t use it as a red-envelope amount, a price or a number you want to show off.

FAQ

What does 514 mean in Chinese?

514 means “‘I’m dying’ (exasperation)” — it is read as 我要死 / 无意思 (wǒ yào sǐ). 5 = 我, 1 = 要, 4 = 死.

Can I use 514 as a red envelope amount?

Avoid for gifts and numbers. Its reading is negative. Don’t use it as a red-envelope amount, a price or a number you want to show off.

How do you pronounce 514?

Digit by digit in Mandarin: wǔ yī sì. The phrase it imitates is 我要死 / 无意思 (wǒ yào sǐ).
