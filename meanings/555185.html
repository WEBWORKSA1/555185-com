What 555185 means

Read as two halves: 555 (呜呜呜, crying) + 185 (要发我, ‘prosperity to me’). The story: stop crying about money — the red packet is on its way. It is a playful, memorable homophone chain and the brand idea behind this website. It contains no 4, has a doubled-up 5 rhythm and ends on the lucky 8-5 ‘prosper-me’ beat.
