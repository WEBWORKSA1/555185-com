What 99 means

9 = 久 (long-lasting). ¥99 or 999 roses signal lasting love. The 9th day of the 9th month is the Double Ninth (Chongyang) festival honouring elders.

FAQ

What does 99 mean in Chinese?

99 means “Long-lasting (forever)” — it is read as 久久 (jiǔ jiǔ). 9 = 久 (long-lasting).

Can I use 99 as a red envelope amount?

Great for gifts, prices & numbers. This is a positive number in Chinese culture — safe and welcome as a red-envelope amount, price ending, phone number or plate.

How do you pronounce 99?

Digit by digit in Mandarin: jiǔ jiǔ. The phrase it imitates is 久久 (jiǔ jiǔ).
