FAQ

What does 286 mean in Chinese?

286 means “Outdated, behind the times” — it is read as 落后 (èr bā liù). A nod to the 1980s Intel 286 computer — calling someone 286 means they are slow or out of touch.

Can I use 286 as a red envelope amount?

Chat slang — fun, not for gifts. It’s internet shorthand. Fine in messages and usernames, but it carries no particular luck as an amount.

How do you pronounce 286?

Digit by digit in Mandarin: èr bā liù. The phrase it imitates is 落后 (èr bā liù).
