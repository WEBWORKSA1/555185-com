What 1314 means

1-3-1-4 sounds like 一生一世 (one life, one world — ‘forever’). Note the 4 here is accepted because the phrase is romantic. ¥1,314 is a classic wedding or anniversary amount.

FAQ

What does 1314 mean in Chinese?

1314 means “For a whole lifetime / forever” — it is read as 一生一世 (yī shēng yī shì). 1-3-1-4 sounds like 一生一世 (one life, one world — ‘forever’).

Can I use 1314 as a red envelope amount?

Romantic — perfect between couples. Use it for anniversaries, 520 Day, proposals and wedding gifts. Less suited to business or elders.

How do you pronounce 1314?

Digit by digit in Mandarin: yī sān yī sì. The phrase it imitates is 一生一世 (yī shēng yī shì).
