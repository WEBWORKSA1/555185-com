Number 4 in Chinese: 四 (sì)

The most avoided digit in Chinese culture because 四 sì sounds like 死 sǐ (death).

Meaning & culture

The most avoided digit in Chinese culture because 四 sì sounds like 死 sǐ (death). Many buildings skip 4th / 14th floors. Never put an amount containing 4 in a red envelope.

⛔ Avoid for gifts and numbers
Its reading is negative. Don’t use it as a red-envelope amount, a price or a number you want to show off.
