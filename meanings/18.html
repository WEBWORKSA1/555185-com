What 18 means

1 (yāo, 要) + 8 (发). Also the age of adulthood. ¥18 / ¥188 are friendly amounts for younger relatives.

18岁生日快乐，要发！
Happy 18th — prosper!
✅ Great for gifts, prices & numbers
This is a positive number in Chinese culture — safe and welcome as a red-envelope amount, price ending, phone number or plate.

FAQ

What does 18 mean in Chinese?

18 means “Will prosper” — it is read as 要发 (yāo fā). 1 (yāo, 要) + 8 (发).

Can I use 18 as a red envelope amount?

Great for gifts, prices & numbers. This is a positive number in Chinese culture — safe and welcome as a red-envelope amount, price ending, phone number or plate.

How do you pronounce 18?

Digit by digit in Mandarin: yī bā. The phrase it imitates is 要发 (yāo fā).
