FAQ

What does the number 5 mean in Chinese?

5 is 五 (wǔ). It sounds like 我 wǒ (I / me) · 呜 wū (crying) · 无 wú (without). 5 is the workhorse of texting slang: it means ‘me’ in 520 (I love you) and ‘boo-hoo’ in 555. It also links to the Five Elements (五行). Neutral in amounts.

Is 5 a lucky number in China?

Neutral. No strong luck either way; meaning comes from the digits around it.
