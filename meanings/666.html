What 666 means

Gamers and live-stream chats spam 666 to praise a skilful move. Unlike the West, 666 is lucky in China (六六大顺 — everything smooth). ¥666 is a popular celebration amount.

这操作太666了！
That move was awesome!
✅ Great for gifts, prices & numbers
This is a positive number in Chinese culture — safe and welcome as a red-envelope amount, price ending, phone number or plate.

FAQ

What does 666 mean in Chinese?

666 means “Awesome! / so smooth / well played” — it is read as 溜溜溜 (liù liù liù). Gamers and live-stream chats spam 666 to praise a skilful move.

Can I use 666 as a red envelope amount?

Great for gifts, prices & numbers. This is a positive number in Chinese culture — safe and welcome as a red-envelope amount, price ending, phone number or plate.

How do you pronounce 666?

Digit by digit in Mandarin: liù liù liù. The phrase it imitates is 溜溜溜 (liù liù liù).
