FAQ

What does 8888 mean in Chinese?

8888 means “Prosperity ×4 (and no 4 in sight)” — it is read as 发发发发 (fā fā fā fā). The ultimate wealth repdigit for big-ticket gifts and business milestones.

Can I use 8888 as a red envelope amount?

Great for gifts, prices & numbers. This is a positive number in Chinese culture — safe and welcome as a red-envelope amount, price ending, phone number or plate.

How do you pronounce 8888?

Digit by digit in Mandarin: bā bā bā bā. The phrase it imitates is 发发发发 (fā fā fā fā).
