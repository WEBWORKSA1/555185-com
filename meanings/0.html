FAQ

What does the number 0 mean in Chinese?

0 is 零 (líng). It sounds like 你 nǐ (you, in texting) · 灵 líng (clever, effective). Zero is a placeholder for ‘you’ in love codes (e.g. 530 我想你). On its own it is neutral; in amounts, round zeros look generous and tidy.

Is 0 a lucky number in China?

Neutral. No strong luck either way; meaning comes from the digits around it.
