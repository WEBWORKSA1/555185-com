What does 185 mean in Chinese?

185 = 要发我 (yāo bā wǔ) — ‘Prosperity — to me!’ (playful reading).

What 185 means

185 is read yāo-bā-wǔ in phone style: 1 = 要 (want / will), 8 = 发 (prosper), 5 = 我 (me). Strung together it gives the cheeky reading 要发我 — ‘prosperity, come to me’ — or ‘send me (a red packet)’. This is a constructed homophone reading, not established slang. Separately, ‘185’ is widely used in Chinese social media as shorthand for a 185 cm (6′1″) height, a popular ideal in dating culture.
