Meaning & culture

In Cantonese 3 (saam) echoes 生 (life), so it is welcome in Hong Kong. In Mandarin some hear 散 (to split up), so couples sometimes avoid it.

FAQ

What does the number 3 mean in Chinese?

3 is 三 (sān). It sounds like 生 shēng (life, birth) · 散 sàn (to scatter / split). In Cantonese 3 (saam) echoes 生 (life), so it is welcome in Hong Kong. In Mandarin some hear 散 (to split up), so couples sometimes avoid it.
