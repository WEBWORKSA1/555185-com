What 88 means

Online, 88 (bā bā) means bye-bye. In money, 88 is double prosperity (发发) — one of the most popular red-envelope amounts across Asia.

FAQ

What does 88 mean in Chinese?

88 means “Bye-bye — or double prosperity” — it is read as 拜拜 / 发发 (bā bā / fā fā). Online, 88 (bā bā) means bye-bye.

Can I use 88 as a red envelope amount?

Great for gifts, prices & numbers. This is a positive number in Chinese culture — safe and welcome as a red-envelope amount, price ending, phone number or plate.

How do you pronounce 88?

Digit by digit in Mandarin: bā bā. The phrase it imitates is 拜拜 / 发发 (bā bā / fā fā).
