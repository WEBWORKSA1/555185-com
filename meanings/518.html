What does 518 mean in Chinese?

518 = 我要发 (wǒ yào fā) — I’m going to prosper.

What 518 means

One of the most coveted business numbers: 5 = 我, 1 = 要, 8 = 发 (prosper). Popular for shop phone lines, plate numbers, prices (¥518) and opening dates (May 18).

开业大吉，518！
Grand opening — I will prosper!
✅ Great for gifts, prices & numbers
This is a positive number in Chinese culture — safe and welcome as a red-envelope amount, price ending, phone number or plate.

FAQ

What does 518 mean in Chinese?

518 means “I’m going to prosper” — it is read as 我要发 (wǒ yào fā). One of the most coveted business numbers: 5 = 我, 1 = 要, 8 = 发 (prosper).

Can I use 518 as a red envelope amount?

Great for gifts, prices & numbers. This is a positive number in Chinese culture — safe and welcome as a red-envelope amount, price ending, phone number or plate.

How do you pronounce 518?

Digit by digit in Mandarin: wǔ yī bā. The phrase it imitates is 我要发 (wǒ yào fā).
