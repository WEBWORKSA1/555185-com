FAQ

What does 584 mean in Chinese?

584 means “I swear” — it is read as 我发誓 (wǒ fā shì). 5 = 我, 8 = 发, 4 = 誓 (shì, oath).

Can I use 584 as a red envelope amount?

Chat slang — fun, not for gifts. It’s internet shorthand. Fine in messages and usernames, but it carries no particular luck as an amount.

How do you pronounce 584?

Digit by digit in Mandarin: wǔ bā sì. The phrase it imitates is 我发誓 (wǒ fā shì).
