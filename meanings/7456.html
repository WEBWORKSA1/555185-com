FAQ

What does 7456 mean in Chinese?

7456 means “I’m so angry / infuriating” — it is read as 气死我了 (qì sǐ wǒ le). 7 = 气 (anger), 4 = 死 (to death), 5 = 我 (me), 6 = 了 (le).

Can I use 7456 as a red envelope amount?

Avoid for gifts and numbers. Its reading is negative. Don’t use it as a red-envelope amount, a price or a number you want to show off.

How do you pronounce 7456?

Digit by digit in Mandarin: qī sì wǔ liù. The phrase it imitates is 气死我了 (qì sǐ wǒ le).
