FAQ

What does 66 mean in Chinese?

66 means “Everything goes smoothly” — it is read as 六六大顺 (liù liù dà shùn). A favourite birthday / exam / new-job amount (¥66, ¥666).

Can I use 66 as a red envelope amount?

Great for gifts, prices & numbers. This is a positive number in Chinese culture — safe and welcome as a red-envelope amount, price ending, phone number or plate.

How do you pronounce 66?

Digit by digit in Mandarin: liù liù. The phrase it imitates is 六六大顺 (liù liù dà shùn).
