Meaning & culture

The luckiest number for wealth: 八 bā rhymes with 发 fā (to prosper, get rich), especially in Cantonese ‘baat / faat’. Beijing opened the 2008 Olympics on 08-08-08 at 8:08 pm.

✅ Great for gifts, prices & numbers
This is a positive number in Chinese culture — safe and welcome as a red-envelope amount, price ending, phone number or plate.

What does the number 8 mean in Chinese?
