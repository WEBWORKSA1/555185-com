What 8013 means

FAQ

What does 8013 mean in Chinese?

8013 means “By your side for a lifetime” — it is read as 伴你一生 (bàn nǐ yī shēng). 8 = 伴 (bàn, accompany), 0 = 你, 1 = 一, 3 = 生.

Can I use 8013 as a red envelope amount?

Romantic — perfect between couples. Use it for anniversaries, 520 Day, proposals and wedding gifts. Less suited to business or elders.

How do you pronounce 8013?

Digit by digit in Mandarin: bā líng yī sān. The phrase it imitates is 伴你一生 (bàn nǐ yī shēng).
