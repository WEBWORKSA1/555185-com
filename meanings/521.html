What 521 means

A companion to 520, often read as 我愿意 (‘I’m willing / I do’). May 21 is a popular wedding-registration and proposal date.

你问我愿不愿意？521！
You ask if I will? 521 — I do!
💞 Romantic — perfect between couples
Use it for anniversaries, 520 Day, proposals and wedding gifts. Less suited to business or elders.

FAQ

What does 521 mean in Chinese?

521 means “I do / I love you” — it is read as 我愿意 / 我爱你 (wǒ yuàn yì / wǒ ài nǐ). A companion to 520, often read as 我愿意 (‘I’m willing / I do’).

Can I use 521 as a red envelope amount?

Romantic — perfect between couples. Use it for anniversaries, 520 Day, proposals and wedding gifts. Less suited to business or elders.

How do you pronounce 521?

Digit by digit in Mandarin: wǔ èr yī. The phrase it imitates is 我愿意 / 我爱你 (wǒ yuàn yì / wǒ ài nǐ).
