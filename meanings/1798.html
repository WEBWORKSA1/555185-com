What 1798 means

1 = 一, 7 = 起 (qǐ), 9 = 走 (zǒu, loosely), 8 = 吧. An invitation to stay together.

1798，不管去哪里。
Let’s go together, wherever.
💞 Romantic — perfect between couples
Use it for anniversaries, 520 Day, proposals and wedding gifts. Less suited to business or elders.

FAQ

What does 1798 mean in Chinese?

1798 means “Let’s go together” — it is read as 一起走吧 (yī qǐ zǒu ba). 1 = 一, 7 = 起 (qǐ), 9 = 走 (zǒu, loosely), 8 = 吧.

Can I use 1798 as a red envelope amount?

Romantic — perfect between couples. Use it for anniversaries, 520 Day, proposals and wedding gifts. Less suited to business or elders.

How do you pronounce 1798?

Digit by digit in Mandarin: yī qī jiǔ bā. The phrase it imitates is 一起走吧 (yī qǐ zǒu ba).
