What 555 means

The single most recognised Chinese texting code for crying. Each 5 (wǔ) mimics the sobbing sound 呜 (wū). It ranges from real sadness to playful whining or mock despair. More fives = more drama: 5555, 55555. Note: in Thai, 555 means ‘hahaha’ — the opposite!
