FAQ

What does 484 mean in Chinese?

484 means “Is it or not? / right?” — it is read as 是不是 (shì bú shì). 4 = 是, 8 = 不 (bù, loosely), 4 = 是.

Can I use 484 as a red envelope amount?

Chat slang — fun, not for gifts. It’s internet shorthand. Fine in messages and usernames, but it carries no particular luck as an amount.

How do you pronounce 484?

Digit by digit in Mandarin: sì bā sì. The phrase it imitates is 是不是 (shì bú shì).
