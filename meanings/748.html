What does 748 mean in Chinese?

What 748 means

7 = 去 (go), 4 = 死 (die), 8 = 吧. A rude brush-off — never use as a gift amount or phone suffix you want to show off.

你走开，748！
Go away, get lost!
⛔ Avoid for gifts and numbers
Its reading is negative. Don’t use it as a red-envelope amount, a price or a number you want to show off.

FAQ

What does 748 mean in Chinese?

Can I use 748 as a red envelope amount?

How do you pronounce 748?
