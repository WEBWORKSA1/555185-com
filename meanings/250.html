What does 250 mean in Chinese?

èr bǎi wǔ — Idiot / fool — never give 250

What 250 means

二百五 is a classic insult meaning a foolish person. Giving ¥250 (or 2,500) in a red envelope is a well-known faux pas — round it to 200, 260 or 288.

你真是个二百五！
You’re such an idiot!
⛔ Avoid for gifts and numbers
Its reading is negative. Don’t use it as a red-envelope amount, a price or a number you want to show off.
