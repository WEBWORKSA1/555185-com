FAQ

What does 888 mean in Chinese?

888 means “Triple prosperity” — it is read as 发发发 (fā fā fā). Wealth, wealth, wealth — one of the most auspicious amounts and prices.

Can I use 888 as a red envelope amount?

Great for gifts, prices & numbers. This is a positive number in Chinese culture — safe and welcome as a red-envelope amount, price ending, phone number or plate.

How do you pronounce 888?

Digit by digit in Mandarin: bā bā bā. The phrase it imitates is 发发发 (fā fā fā).
