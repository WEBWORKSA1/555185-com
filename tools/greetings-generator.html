How to use Chinese greetings

Say or write the blessing when you hand over a red envelope, at a wedding banquet toast, or in a WeChat message. Pair 恭喜发财 (gōngxǐ fācái — wishing you prosperity) with a red envelope at New Year; write 百年好合 on a wedding envelope; use 生意兴隆 for a business opening.

2027 is the Year of the Goat (羊年, from 6 February 2027). The classic pun is 三羊开泰 (sān yáng kāi tài), a play on 三阳开泰 — “three yang bring peace and prosperity”.

FAQ

What do you say when giving a red envelope?

The most common phrase is 恭喜发财 (gōngxǐ fācái), ‘wishing you prosperity’. Children often reply 恭喜发财，红包拿来 — ‘prosperity to you, now hand over the red packet!’

Is ‘Gong Hei Fat Choy’ the same thing?

Yes — it’s the Cantonese pronunciation of 恭喜发财, common in Hong Kong, Guangdong and many overseas communities.

What greeting fits the Year of the Goat?

羊年大吉 (yáng nián dà jí — great luck in the Year of the Goat) and 三羊开泰 (sān yáng kāi tài) are perfect for 2027.
