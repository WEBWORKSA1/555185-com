Quick answers

What does 555185 mean?

Read in two halves: 555 (wǔ wǔ wǔ ≈ 呜呜呜) is Chinese texting slang for crying, and 185 (yāo bā wǔ ≈ 要发我) is a playful reading of ‘prosperity — to me!’. Together: from tears to fortune. It is a constructed homophone reading, not an official term.

How much money goes in a red envelope?

It depends on region and relationship: roughly ¥100–200 for friends’ children in mainland China, HK$20–50 in Hong Kong, S$8–10 in Singapore and $10–20 in North America; parents receive much more. Our free planner calculates lucky amounts for your whole list.

When is Lunar New Year 2027?

Saturday, 6 February 2027 — the start of the Year of the Fire Goat.
